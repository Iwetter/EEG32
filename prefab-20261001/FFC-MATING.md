# FFC mating specification — harness H1, revision 2.1

**Cable:** Molex **0151660431** (15166-0431), 40 conductors, **0.50 mm pitch**, **102 ±3 mm length**, **Type A, same-side exposed contacts**. Opposite-side Type D is not the specified cable. Connector End A is the existing EEG32 J601 **Molex 503480-4000**. For the bench harness, End B shall use another **503480-4000**, designated H1/JB on a separate passive breakout, wired by circuit number as specified below. The breakout is an interface contract; this checkpoint does not include a newly designed remote PCB or an approved electrode termination. [Cable product](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/0151660431), [cable sales drawing, SD-15166-001 rev F](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/salesdrawingpdf/151/15166/151660097_sd.pdf), [connector drawing](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/salesdrawingpdf/503/503480/5034802200_sd.pdf).

**Fit qualification hold:** cable end thickness is specified as 0.30 ±0.05 mm, while this 40-contact connector requires 0.30 ±0.03 mm. The cable's full production tolerance is therefore not automatically compatible. Accept only cable ends measured within **0.270–0.330 mm** over the stiffened insertion area, with measurement uncertainty inside those limits, or obtain a supplier-qualified tighter-tolerance lot/approved alternative. Measure both ends without deforming them. Do not force an out-of-range tail, scrape its stiffener or assume nominal 0.30 mm guarantees fit.

Use the [orientation drawing](FFC-orientation.svg) in its common, flat top-view convention. The EEG32 board is below End A, cable extends toward the top edge (KiCad −Y), and the cable has no twist. Its exposed contacts face the viewer at **both ends**; stiffeners face away. The conductor-1 edge is the **right-hand edge along the whole flat cable**. End A's tip points downward toward J601; End B's tip points upward toward JB. If each end is instead inspected separately with its tip pointing away and contacts visible, conductor 1 is on the left at End A and on the right at End B. These are the same physical conductor viewed after a rotation.

On the saved EEG32 top view, J601 pad **1** is **(46.750, 14.325) mm**, at the right-hand end of the contact row; pad 40 is **(27.250, 14.325) mm**. The footprint is at (37.000, 13.000) mm, rotation 180°. A printed `1` sits beside its right end. The separate JB is front-mounted, using the same local footprint at **0°**, mouth toward End A (common-view downward). Its **circuit 1 is on the left**, circuit 40 on the right. Therefore End B conductor 1 mates to JB circuit 40. This reverses physical connector circuit numbers while preserving all EEG32 signal labels through the explicitly labeled breakout. Verify numbering from its drawing and the actual part before designing the remote PCB.

The cable has no guaranteed printed conductor-1 stripe. Establish conductor 1 by continuity from J601 circuit 1 on an unpowered fixture, then place durable `1 / A` and `1 / B` marks on the corresponding outer insulation/stiffener edges outside the insertion/contact area. Blue stiffener color is not a pin-number reference. Open each actuator, insert the tail squarely to the specified seated position, and close it using the Molex procedure. Provide mechanical strain relief outside the connector and keep bending/pulling away from the seated tail.

[FFC-end-to-end.csv](FFC-end-to-end.csv) lists every conductor individually, including the NC conductors. Cable conductor identity is straight-through, but the specified facing connectors give the acceptance circuit map **J601 n → JB (41−n)**. For example J601.1→JB.40 is CH01, J601.32→JB.9 is CH32. Do not apply an assumed connector n→n map. Check all 40 conductors with the loose harness, then recheck 1–34 to the assembled breakout and EEG32 unpowered board. On a loose harness, a fixture-subtracted continuity resistance ≤2 Ω per conductor is the project acceptance limit; no unintended adjacent/nonadjacent shorts may be detected. Insulation qualification must follow the cable/connector rating and safety review, and must not apply a megohmmeter voltage to a populated AFE.

Record cable lot, both measured tail thicknesses, orientation marks, full continuity result, actuator condition and mating count. Limit planned mating to the connector's 10-cycle rating; use a replaceable breakout for repeated testing. Failure to pass thickness, numbering or contact checks blocks harness release. The electrode model, patient-side termination and protection/isolation remain supervisor/safety review inputs.
