# BOM specification and qualification

[BOM.csv](BOM.csv) is the revision 2.1 procurement list. All 225 unique PCB references carry Manufacturer, MPN, Electrical_Spec, Datasheet and Qualification fields in the schematic and PCB. Of these, 213 are fitted components and 12 are bare PCB features (P1, P2, TP1–TP10), explicitly marked NOT PURCHASED and excluded from placement. ADS1299 multi-unit symbols count once per physical device. Native schematic BOM settings include the procurement fields.

Nominal values, package geometry and placement remain unchanged. Previously specified matching, oscillator, IC and input-filter parts were retained. Generic capacitors/resistors and the 15 nH choke now have exact ordering parts. Taiyo Yuden C5/C9 use the manufacturer's renamed MSAST105SB5104KFNA01 identity for TMK105BJ104KV-F, at the existing 100 nF/25 V/X5R/0402 specification. [Manufacturer part record](https://ds.yuden.co.jp/TYCOMPAS/ap/detail?pn=MSAST105SB5104KFNA01&u=I).

Requirements derive from the manufacturers: [ABLIC S-1318](https://www.ablic.com/en/doc/datasheet/voltage_regulator/S1318_E.pdf), [TI ADS1299](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads1299.pdf), [Torex XC9140](https://product.torexsemi.com/system/files/series/xc9140.pdf), [ECS HF](https://ecsxtal.com/store/pdf/ECX-1637.pdf), [ECS LF](https://ecsxtal.com/store/pdf/ECX-12.pdf), [Murata L1](https://search.murata.co.jp/Ceramy/image/img/P02/JELF243A-9138.pdf), and each BOM row's manufacturer record. Specifications define procurement targets; stock availability, lifecycle, lot authenticity and MLCC bias curves still require buyer/vendor confirmation. F1 retains the legacy 2450FM07A0029T ordering code; the [manufacturer's current global code](https://www.johansontechnology.com/products/integrated-passives/low-pass-filters/2450fm07a0029001t/) is 2450FM07A0029001T. Require supplier identity/land verification before substituting it. Some direct PDF downloads failed; [source-manifest.json](sources/source-manifest.json) records failures rather than representing them as archived evidence.

Release the MLCC choices only after obtaining exact-part vendor curves or measured capacitance at operating bias, temperature and aging limits. Derate tantalum parts for the actual rail/transients. Any failed qualification should return a concrete datasheet violation and proposed compatible correction for review. Do not replace parts merely to optimize price/stock, and do not treat a filled MPN field as completed performance qualification.
