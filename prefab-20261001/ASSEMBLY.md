# Assembly instructions — 2.1 pre-fab bench checkpoint

Use [BOM.csv](BOM.csv), [front centroids](centroids-front.csv), [bottom centroids](centroids-back.csv), [front drawing](assembly-front.pdf) and [bottom drawing](assembly-back.pdf) together. There are **212 top-side fitted parts and one bottom-side part, C15**, for 213 fitted parts total. P1/P2 and TP1–TP10 are bare solder/test pads. Their inherited schematic value `Header 2` does not mean a purchased header; do not populate these footprints with through-hole headers.

Centroids use **mm**, native KiCad footprint origins, absolute PCB coordinates: CSV PosX=KiCad X and PosY=−KiCad Y. Rot is KiCad's exported rotation; Side is top/bottom. No extra bottom-X negation has been applied. Do not infer package pin 1 from centroid rotation alone. Match the assembly drawings and manufacturer tape orientation; the assembler must validate its import convention with first-article placement before applying reel rotations. [orientation-pads.csv](orientation-pads.csv) gives actual pin-1/polarized-pad coordinates in the native positive-Y KiCad coordinate system. The bottom PDF is mirrored as a physical bottom view; the centroid file must not be mirrored a second time.

U1 is Nordic QFN48, 6×6 mm. Its **4.6×4.6 mm exposed VSS pad** must be soldered. The existing reference footprint has a single full-size front paste aperture; this revision preserves it. Specify an initial **0.10 mm stencil** for assembler review. U1's existing six through vias beneath the exposed pad are now explicitly marked **filled and capped** in the PCB. Fabricate them with resin fill and copper caps/planarization on both sides; plain tenting or soldermask plugging is insufficient beneath a soldered exposed pad. [exposed-pad-vias.csv](exposed-pad-vias.csv) is the exact location/size list. Confirm the fabricator supports those sizes and that the solderable EP surface is flat.

Before assembly release, the assembler must review the full EP aperture against its paste, stencil and reflow process. X-ray the first articles for EP wetting, voiding, peripheral solder joints, floating/skew and bridges. Record the agreed void/coverage criterion and reflow profile. No unsupported universal void percentage is invented here. If a segmented stencil is required, obtain an assembler-qualified stencil-only proposal and document its area/release criteria before changing the saved aperture. Nordic's reference footprint is retained; process qualification remains open.

Printed front circles identify pin 1 of U1, U101, U201, U301 and U401. A front `1` identifies the J601 circuit-1 end. Printed `+` marks identify the anode ends of C101/C201/C301/C401; the tantalum manufacturer's polarity marking must align with pad 1. U101/U201 have pin 1 at their upper-right package side in the common board view; U301/U401 have pin 1 at their lower-left side. U1 pin 1 is the upper end of its left row. Small U2/U3 and the RF/crystal parts use the manufacturer drawing plus the Fab-layer/pin coordinate table; no speculative reel rotation is supplied. Verify F1 IN1/OUT3/GND2/4 and all keyed/polarized package orientations on the first article.

C15 is the only bottom component, centered at **(67.300, 29.620) mm**, rotation 0°; pad 1 (+3V3) is (66.800,29.620) mm. It is a nonpolar 0402 bypass capacitor beneath the MCU area. A bottom `C15` mark identifies it. Plan double-side reflow or controlled hand assembly with an appropriate support nest. Keep the fixture clear of C15 and filled/capped via surfaces; protect the top FFC actuator and tall parts when flipping/reworking. Do not omit C15. Keep the phantom/electrode connector disconnected during assembly and programming.

SWD fixture: use four top pogo contacts on **TP1 GND, TP2 +3V3/VTref, TP3 SWDCLK, TP4 SWDIO**. [fixture-pads.csv](fixture-pads.csv) supplies all ten 1.5 mm test-pad coordinates. Connect GND first, use TP2 as the probe's voltage reference, and power the board through P2 (pad 1 GND, pad 2 VBAT) using a current-limited supply or the approved battery. Avoid simultaneous probe-power and battery sources/backfeeding. P1 provides optional solder-wire access to SWDCLK/SWDIO only; it has neither GND nor VTref. There is no dedicated MCU reset test pad; use normal SWD attachment and a controlled power cycle when needed. An under-reset fixture would require a separately reviewed reset access change.

Provide a keyed mechanical nest/edge references, short pogo paths, retention, cable strain relief and clearance for C15. Panel tooling holes/fiducials belong on assembler-approved rails; do not drill the board or modify its analog/RF area to create fixture access. Program/read device ID and FICR lot evidence, verify all rails, and confirm P0.25/P0.26 are never driven. TP5 is AFE_CLK, TP6 START, TP7 filtered REF, TP8 BIAS_DRIVE, TP9 +5VA, TP10 VBAT. Instrument connections are for phantom-only bench testing until the safety review is complete.

Molex's connector drawing specifies a **0.10 mm metal mask** with 0.7×0.3 mm terminal and 1.0×0.3 mm nail apertures; verify the saved connector geometry and seating/reflow profile before production. [Molex connector drawing](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/salesdrawingpdf/503/503480/5034802200_sd.pdf). Approve panelization, stencil, paste/reflow, first-article orientation/X-ray and the single bottom part before assembly release.
